Le Hong Trang

Associate Professor
Faculty of Computer Science & Engineering, HCMUT

Le Hong Trang is an Associate Professor at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), Vietnam, where he leads a research group for Trustworthy & Generalizable AI. His research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, game theory, machine learning, and optimization, with current interests in trustworthy and cooperative AI, intelligent planning, and learning-based methods for industrial applications. Alongside his academic research, he has substantial industry experience in developing AI solutions for industrial manufacturing.

Previously, he received his Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from the University of Lisboa, Portugal, in 2014, including a one-year research stay at KU Leuven, Belgium. In 2024, he was a USA-ID PHER Visiting Scholar at California State University, Northridge, USA.
