TRANG's Lab — Transformative Research in Artificial Intelligence & Generalization — is a research group directed by Prof. [PI Name], working on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and computer vision, at Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering, Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT). Our current research focuses on generalization and robustness, foundation models, open-world learning, generative AI, and their relevant fields. The overarching goal of our research is to build AI systems that generalize beyond their training distribution — principled, reliable, and transformative in real-world deployment.

Research areas: Generalization & Robustness Open-World Learning Foundation Models Generative AI Computer Vision Vision-Language Models Trustworthy / Agentic AI
Openings
